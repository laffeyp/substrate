description: Rewrite prose plainly and remove machine-sounding words.
---

Rewrite the text in four passes.

1. Delete: every sentence that adds no fact; every restatement.
2. Specify: replace each vague word with the fact it hides. "Robust" becomes what survives which
   failure; "comprehensive" becomes the count.
3. Remove these words and shapes: delve, underscore, showcase, boast, foster, garner, pivotal,
   crucial, robust, comprehensive, seamless, innovative, intricate, meticulous, realm, tapestry,
   landscape, journey, vibrant, "plays a vital role", "stands as a testament", "it's worth
   noting", "in conclusion", a closing summary, every list exactly three items, bold on every term.
4. Vary: mix short and long sentences; turn lists back into prose where it reads better.

Keep the facts. Use short words, concrete nouns, active verbs.
