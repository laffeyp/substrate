# Guidance levels compared (2026-10-10)

*The three standard levels side by side, as shipped in `substrate/src/substrate/topologies/session/bundles/` (kernel K287, K288). Each row is one topic; a dash means the level leaves it out. Head sizes: high 2,995 tokens, medium 1,218, low 619. The reminder line is the same for every level; low drops the two writers' names.*

**Every level, the default `harness` level included, also carries the harness rules** (`bundles/harness/methodology.md`):

> Work through the request. Read the tools before you call one. When a
> tool returns, read the result before your next step; when a tool
> fails, read the error and correct the next call, do not repeat the
> same call blind.
> >
> > Say what you did. Do not claim work you did not do. When the task is
> finished and verified, stop.

| **Sources are claims** | Training data and the web are full of marketing, astroturf, recycled press releases and consensus that is only the loudest claim repeated.
