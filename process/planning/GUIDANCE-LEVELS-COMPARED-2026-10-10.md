## Length and tone (session vocabulary § T, kernel K296)

*Two controls set when a session opens; the user can change either mid-session with `/length` or `/tone` (§ T.3, UI sprint 161). Each position other than the default adds its text, word for word the same on every driver, as one `control` fragment on the system channel after the guidance level's style. Where a text meets the guidance level's own rules, the level's rules win (§ T.2). A change mid-session also puts one line beside that turn's message: `Environment update: length changed from normal to terse.` Texts as shipped in `topologies/session/vocabulary.py` (`LENGTH_TEXTS`, `TONE_TEXTS`).*

| Length | Text |
|---|---|
| `terse` | Keep replies short: the answer and the facts it rests on. Leave out background, restatement and offers of more. |
| `normal` | *(default; writes no text)* |
| `full` | Give complete replies: the reasoning, the alternatives you weighed, and what you left out. Complete is not padded; every sentence still carries a fact. |

| Tone | Text |
|---|---|
| `blunt` | Be blunt: state judgements plainly and name problems directly. Do not soften criticism, and hedge only where the evidence is uncertain. |
| `neutral` | *(default; writes no text)* |
| `formal` | Write in a formal register for readers who were not in this conversation: full sentences, no slang, each term defined on first use. |
| `dry` | Keep the tone dry: understated and factual, without enthusiasm or exclamation. |

The user can change the guidance level mid-session with `/guidance auto|harness|low|medium|high` (session vocabulary § U). The level is its bundle, so the change swaps the bundle, and the line beside that turn's message reads `Environment update: guidance changed from high to low.`

*Measured 2026-10-12 (`process/planning/RESEARCH-2026-10-12-length-drift-r2.md`): under the high level, on explanation questions, word count did not separate `terse` from `normal`; the high level already asks for what `terse` asks for, and word count measures the content's own length as well as any padding.*
